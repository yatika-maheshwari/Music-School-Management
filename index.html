About Serenova

Serenova Music School, a place where creativity, passion and music come together. We believe that music is more than just learning an instrument; it is a way of expressing yourself. Our academy provides a supportive learning environment for beginners as well as aspiring musicians.

Our User Roles

Sere provides a role-based management system designed to make music education organised and accessible.

Administrator

Responsible for managing the academy, including students, teachers, courses, schedules and overall operations.

Teachers

See your Students, approve or reject class requests, and mark classes completed.

Students

Can explore courses, enroll in classes, view schedules and access personal learning information.
Each user receives access to features according to their assigned role, ensuring an organised and efficient experience.
